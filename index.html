What Three Cities Is

A club, not a venue. A community, not a customer list.

People are lonelier and more online than ever — and starving for a place that feels like theirs. Three Cities is the third place: not home, not work, but the room where members actually know each other. The space is the stage. The community is the product.

Membership at the core

Recurring dues create a committed base and predictable revenue — a community that shows up, not foot traffic that hopes to. Members join a roster, not a mailing list.

The Franchise Model

What you bring. What we hand you.

A Three Cities franchise is a partnership. You bring the local roots and the drive to run it. We bring the brand, the blueprint, and a team that has already done it once.

What you bring

The right operator in the right city
  • → Capital to fund the build-out and opening runway
  • → Deep local roots and a network in your market
  • → A full-time commitment to being the operator on the ground
  • → Hospitality or community-building instincts
  • → Alignment with the Three Cities values and standards

What we hand you

The full Three Cities system
  • ✦ The brand license and exclusive rights to your territory
  • ✦ The complete eight-system operating playbook
  • ✦ Site-selection and build-out support with our design kit
  • ✦ The technology stack, pre-configured for your location
  • ✦ Hands-on launch support and founding-member campaign
  • ✦ Training, ongoing coaching, and a network of operators

Questions

The things everyone asks.

Do I need experience in hospitality or nightlife?+

It helps, but it's not a hard requirement. What matters most is that you're a natural community builder, a committed operator, and deeply connected to your city. The playbook supplies the hospitality systems; you supply the drive and the local roots.

What kind of investment does it take?+

It varies by market and space, and the full investment range and unit economics are shared with qualified applicants under NDA during discovery. In broad terms, you should be prepared to fund the build-out plus an operating runway to carry the location through its founding-member ramp.

Do I get an exclusive territory?+

Yes. Each operator gets defined, exclusive rights to their market. We're building a network of great clubs, not saturating cities — protecting your territory protects the brand and your investment.

How long until I open?+

Most operators move from signed agreement to opening night in roughly six to twelve months, depending on how quickly you secure and build out a space. The full path is laid out in the Path to Open section above.

What happened to Wicker Park?+

We made a deliberate choice to concentrate on River North as the flagship — the single, focused proving ground for the model. That discipline is exactly what makes the playbook franchise-ready: one room, perfected, and built to replicate.

Can I open more than one location?+

Absolutely. Once you've proven you can run the model, there's a path to multi-unit development within and beyond your territory. Our best operators are the ones we want to grow with.
